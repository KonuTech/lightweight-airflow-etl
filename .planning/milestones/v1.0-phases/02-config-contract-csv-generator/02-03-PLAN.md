<objective>
Port the reference repo's manifest + digest-oracle byte-level fixture-corpus architecture
(`tools/corpus/`, D-16) — scoped to this project's structural/type/nullability-only validation
domain, never importing `dataplat` — and prove it end-to-end with the first fixture category
(dialect/encoding, 8 fixtures). This is a separate, parallel subsystem from Plans 01-02's
config/generator work: it has zero data or config dependency on `csv_processor`, and exists purely
to give Phase 3's future Tier-A vendored `detect/*`/`compression.py` modules byte-exact fixtures to
be tested against, verified by a committed SHA-256 digest oracle rather than trusted on faith.

Purpose: establish the corpus's determinism mechanism (the ten R1-R10 rules from the reference
repo's ADR-0005) and its CLI/Makefile scaffolding before Plans 04-05 scale it up to the full
~30-fixture set.
Output: a working `tools/corpus` package, `make fixtures`/`make fixtures-verify` Makefile targets,
and 8 committed dialect/encoding fixture definitions with a passing digest-oracle round-trip.
</objective>
